# A little room to think.

Markdown, without the distractions. **Folio** gives your words a quiet place to land — and keeps the source one click away.

## Read beautifully. Edit lightly.

Switch between **Preview**, **Edit**, and **Split** in the toolbar. Your document stays a plain Markdown file, right where you left it.

- Open a file with **⌘O**, or drop it into the window.
- Save your changes with **⌘S**.
- Find a passage with **⌘F**.
- Use **⌘1**, **⌘2**, and **⌘3** to switch views.

> The best tool is the one that leaves you alone with your ideas.

## The details matter

Tables, task lists, links, local images, and syntax-highlighted code — rendered natively, without a browser.

| View | For |
| :--- | :--- |
| Preview | Reading and reviewing |
| Edit | A quick change to the source |
| Split | Seeing your words take shape |
